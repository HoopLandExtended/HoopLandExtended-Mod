# Hoop Land Extended (HLE)

**0.5.1-alpha.6 · Windows x64 · Steam · Hoop Land 1.12.1**

Hoop Land Extended adds more ways to develop your players, run your team, and follow what's happening around your league. It expands Skills, Legendary Abilities, Athleticism, professional finances, training, mentorship, fans, and social features, with settings that let you choose what you want to use.

Alpha.6 brings clearer trade details, improved Player Control and Recommendations, smoother Finance menus, and a fix for paid Career facility upgrades getting stuck between seasons.

[Download HLE](https://github.com/HoopLandExtended/HoopLandExtended-Mod/releases) · [Release notes](Documentation/Release-Notes.md) · [Report a bug](https://github.com/HoopLandExtended/HoopLandExtended-Mod/issues)

## Requirements

- Your own Steam copy of **Hoop Land 1.12.1**.
- **64-bit Windows 10 or 11**.
- Internet access for the first setup.

Hoop Land is not included. Android and iOS are not supported by this release.

## Install and play

1. Download **HLE-0.5.1-alpha.6-Launcher-Windows.zip** from the release's **Assets** section. Choose the HLE release ZIP rather than GitHub's automatically generated source-code archives.
2. Extract the whole download into a folder outside your Steam game folder.
3. Open **HLE.exe**, select your updated Steam Hoop Land folder, and choose **Set Up HLE**.
4. When setup finishes, choose **Play HLE**. Use **Settings** to choose your features.

Keep **HLE.exe** and **HLE_Data** together. The launcher handles installation, updates, settings, repair, and launching the game.

HLE creates its own game copy and leaves your Steam installation alone. **It shares Hoop Land's save location**, so back up your careers before updating or trying an alpha.

## Updating

1. Close Hoop Land and the HLE launcher.
2. Extract the new release into your existing HLE folder. Replace the included files and keep the rest of **HLE_Data**.
3. Open **HLE.exe** and choose **Update HLE**. Select your updated Steam game folder if asked.

Updates preserve your settings and compatible HLE progress. If you'd rather keep your old installation handy, set up a separate HLE folder, then use **Tools > Import Previous HLE State**. Keep the old folder until you've checked your progress.

## What's included

- **Skills, Legendary Abilities, and Athleticism:** expanded player options with descriptions, requirements, costs, and equipped states in the player menus.
- **Pro League finances:** choose the Soft Salary Cap, luxury-tax threshold, First Apron, and Second Apron individually. Finance explains cap room, Bird rights, exceptions, projected tax, and completed charges.
- **Front-office control:** use Automatic, Player Control, or Recommendations for your supported managed team. Other teams continue to make decisions.
- **Market Activity:** follow proposals and decisions through League News. Trade details show the assets, annual salary coming in and going out, and the change to payroll. Completed moves remain in the game's Transactions section.
- **Training and mentorship:** choose team plans and eligible mentor pairings, then see completed gains and highlights in Development.
- **Optional Persistent Form:** earned hot and cold streaks appear beside players in roster and player views, with details about their current Form.
- **Optional Instant Practice:** complete supported practice opportunities quickly while keeping their normal costs and usage limits.
- **In-game UI:** a shared native-style presentation with clearer action labels.

## Game modes

| Mode | HLE behavior |
| --- | --- |
| Pro Career | Player progression and supported team management, Finance, and development features. Your player's personal contract decisions remain yours. |
| College Career and Campus | College finances stay with Hoop Land. HLE Finance runs for the background Pro League, which you can view through the game's league switch. |
| Franchise | Finance and development follow your managed team. |
| Commissioner | Pro financial AI runs across the league; choose a team explicitly for manual management and development actions. |
| Exhibition and Shootaround | Applicable match features follow the players on the court without advancing an unrelated career's finances or development. |

Features apply where the mode supports them; a match-only mode doesn't have the same progression or management systems as a career.

## Settings and saves

Fresh installations use **Automatic** front-office decisions, training, and mentoring. **Persistent Form** and **Instant Practice** start off and can be enabled independently. Updating keeps your existing choices.

**Save and roster editing remains supported.** If you edit financial contracts, close the game and use **Tools > Import Edited Financial Save** so HLE's financial records can catch up. See [Save and roster editing](Documentation/Save-Editing.md) for the steps.

## Help and feedback

**Instructions.pdf** covers the quick installation steps. The full user guide is included in the release's **Documentation** folder and can be opened through the launcher.

This is still an alpha. Physical-controller navigation, Escape input, every screen size, and every alternate draft or offseason route haven't all been checked. Game updates may also need a newer HLE build.

If you find a problem, use **Tools > Collect Report** and open a [GitHub issue](https://github.com/HoopLandExtended/HoopLandExtended-Mod/issues). Include your HLE and game versions, game mode, what happened, and how to reproduce it. Review the report before posting it; it can include local paths and gameplay details.

For installation problems, **Tools** also includes **Repair / Reinstall HLE** and access to backups.
